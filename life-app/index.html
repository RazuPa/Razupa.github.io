BUILT AROUND PROGRESSION

Make everyday actions feel rewarding.

Life App combines activity tracking with game mechanics designed to make consistent progress visible and engaging.

✦

Earn XP

Complete activities such as running, studying, reading or coding and earn XP for your progress.

↑

Level Up

Build lifetime XP, increase your level and turn consistent habits into visible progression.

✓

Complete Quests

Set goals and work toward quests that add structure to your everyday activities.

◆

Unlock Achievements

Reach milestones and unlock achievements based on your progress.

◉

Build Your Buddy

Your Buddy grows alongside your real-world activity, connecting player progression with the game world.

⚔

Explore the World

Enter Buddy World, explore locations and battle enemies using progress earned outside the game.
